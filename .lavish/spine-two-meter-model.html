The Unseen Hand · Direction Notes · Set 1

The Spine, refined:
a tightrope between two damnations

Your Q1.2 quietly changed the whole game. The old design had one failure mode. The new one has two, on opposite ends of a single gauge, and the skill is never touching either.

The core tension, visualized

// FAITH drifts on world outcomes — both ends are a loss

Hated · 0

Adventurers turn on god. They resist interference and actively hunt you. Bad end: they fight you.

The sustainable middle

Loved enough to steer, not so adored they lose their minds.

Worshipped · max

Fanatic cultists roam the world, sacrificing and cannibalising to drag god into being. Bad end: delusion warps the world.

▼ Bad outcomes drag faith down — whether or not you intervened.
▲ Favourable outcomes nudge faith up.
▲▲ Miracles (big DI spends) spike faith fast — the cultist trap.

The untangling: two meters, not one

// Q1.2 blends a spendable tool with a reputation gauge — they must be distinct

Your tool

Divine Influence

Is
A spendable currency for interventions (the original one).
Moves
Trickles in passively; you burn it on nudges, touches, seeded events.
At zero
You simply can't intervene until it refills. Not a loss state.
Your tightrope

Faith / Favour

Is
A reputation gauge the world holds toward god.
Moves
Drifts on world outcomes — good, bad, and miraculous — not on your button-presses directly.
At extremes
Triggers the two bad ends. This is the meter you're really managing.
The elegant hook: a big DI spend is exactly what creates a miracle, and a miracle is exactly what shoves Faith toward the cultist cliff. Your tool and your tightrope are wired together — power used loudly is power that damns you.

Why this is a stronger spine

// the pivot, stated plainly

Old tension

One-sided. Spend DI carefully, let the world breathe, grow stronger. The only failure was DI bankruptcy from over-saving.

becomes
New tension

Two-sided. Neglect drops faith toward hatred; over-intervention spikes it toward fanaticism. Both extremes end you. The game lives in the middle.
