Social interactions
socialResolver.ts · IDLE/RESTING only

So the real gap isn't "no way to move points" - it's coverage, frequency, and visibility. Social ticks only fire for idle/resting pairs, deltas are quiet, and nothing surfaces the change to you. Plus a hard boundary: notable NPCs can bond with adventurers, but NPC↔NPC relationships are explicitly out of scope (socialResolver.ts:494) - which is exactly what your "townsfolk know each other" wish needs. That reframes this from build-from-scratch to extend + surface + decide the NPC boundary.

The work-now cluster

// three interlocking systems — relationships first (it's the #1 want)

1
The #1 new thing

Social relationship events

Expand the drivers (gifts, betrayals, shared danger, rivalry), bring service NPCs into the model, seed townsfolk with prior familiarity, and surface every shift in the feed so the player feels the web move.

2
Makes #1 believable

Deeper character autonomy

Richer per-character models - likes, dislikes, detailed goals - so social and quest choices stop feeling scheduled. This is what makes relationship events read as character-driven, not dice-driven.

3
Stop breaking the flow

Non-disruptive decision moments

Rework choice cards so they inform without yanking control - rethink whether (and when) they pause the world. The spine wants a world that keeps breathing while you watch.
