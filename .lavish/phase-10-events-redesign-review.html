I The Two Rulings

Two forks where the fresh /lavish direction collided with the older, still-unbuilt P9 social plans. You ruled on both; here is what each ruling actually changes.

Narrator & Voice — decision #1 vs the cancelled P9c

Rejected (P9c)

The LLM writes text for every social scene (batched Haiku calls). Each character carries a CharacterContextCard that persists memory. The day-summary narrator is deleted.

➜
Chosen (decision #1)

A deterministic subject + beat + colour template grammar renders the feed. The LLM is reserved for three set-pieces only. The day-summary narrator is kept.

Why: per-event LLM cost & latency — and it would break feed replay determinism. Templates are load-bearing; the LLM is a spotlight, not the stage lighting.

Social Timing — decision #2 vs the cancelled P9b

Rejected (P9b)

A memoryless per-tick coin-flip: p = BASE × empathyMult × relationshipMult × compatibilityMult, rolled fresh every tick.

➜
Chosen (decision #2)

A pressure accumulator: tension builds from proximity + mood-strain + relationship-friction, discharges at a jittered moment past a threshold, then a post-fire cooldown.

Why: tension that is "due" releases organically instead of snapping randomly; reuses the existing decisionCooldowns pattern.

II Two Deliberate Consequences

Both follow directly from ruling #1 — flagging them so nothing is a surprise later.

Dropped

The character cards are gone

CharacterContextCard is removed entirely. No per-character LLM memory. Continuity is the history layer + the day summary. The panel's "Last Day Events" now reads the deterministic event/history record instead of a card.

Kept

The day-summary narrator survives

llm-narrator.md is un-superseded and reframed as set-piece #1 (with decision-moment framing & quest-climax). Per-event / per-scene LLM is now explicitly forbidden in packages/core.
